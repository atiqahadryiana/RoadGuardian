Why explore 5G?

Future deployments may benefit from fast distribution of corroborated events across many roadside nodes. Latency, coverage, reliability, and safety outcomes must be independently field-tested against 4G and wired alternatives.

Concept architecture · Not implemented

A privacy-first signal path, designed for human oversight.

The prototype communicates a possible future workflow. It does not run an AI model, connect to 5G, identify people, control traffic lights, or contact responders.

  1. 01
    Sense

    Permissioned roadside sensors produce minimal, non-identifying observations.

  2. 02
    Corroborate

    Validated edge models and safety rules compare multiple signals.

  3. 03
    Distribute

    Secure networks share verified event metadata with resilient fallback.

  4. 04
    Review

    Authorised operators assess context before any real-world response.

Malaysia-first context

Mixed motorcycle traffic, tropical rain, school crossings, last-mile transit links, and staged deployment economics.

Privacy by design

No camera stream, facial recognition, plate recognition, geolocation collection, personal profile, or cloud transmission in this demo.

Safe deployment path

Begin with authority approval, shadow-mode evaluation, independent validation, security review, fallback planning, and measured field trials.
