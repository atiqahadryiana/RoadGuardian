# Innovation canvas

## Challenge

Mixed motorcycle traffic, tropical downpours, busy school crossings, and last-mile transport interchanges can create rapidly changing road conditions. Operators may receive fragmented signals and limited shared context.

## Proposed concept

RoadGuardian AI explores a privacy-first roadside intelligence network that could corroborate minimal sensor observations near the edge, distribute verified event metadata, and give authorised operators a shared view. The current prototype only simulates that workflow.

## Malaysia-first differentiation

- Scenarios centre motorcycles, tropical rain, education zones, crossings, and transit-linked last-mile travel.
- A staged, read-only pilot path acknowledges coverage variability and public-budget constraints.
- Privacy principles avoid identity as a prerequisite for road-risk awareness.

## Responsible role for 5G

5G is framed as a candidate distribution layer for many roadside nodes and edge workflows, not as a guaranteed safety outcome. Real adoption depends on independent comparison of latency, reliability, coverage, security, resilience, and total cost against alternatives.

## Value hypothesis — unvalidated

- Earlier shared context for authorised operators.
- Lower raw-data movement through edge filtering and metadata-first design.
- More consistent review and audit of emerging road-risk signals.

These are hypotheses, not measured results or crash-prevention claims.

## Proof-of-concept evidence

The demo shows five scenarios, a changing junction schematic, simulated risk and latency, mock sensor status, an actionable local incident queue, persistence, filters, and CSV export. It demonstrates interaction coherence and safety communication, not technical performance.

## Pilot path

1. Authority and community discovery, data-protection assessment, and hazard analysis.
2. Offline replay with approved, de-identified data and independently defined metrics.
3. One-junction shadow mode with no driver alert, dispatch, or actuation.
4. Independent technical, accessibility, security, and safety review.
5. Only then, consider a tightly governed information-only trial with fallback and human oversight.

## Success criteria for a future study

Measure false alerts, missed events, operator comprehension, end-to-end availability, network variability, privacy leakage, accessibility, total cost, and safe-degradation behavior. Do not infer crash reduction from demo interactions.
